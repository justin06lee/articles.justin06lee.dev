## Things
What is nothing, and what is everything? Let's imagine the vastness of these terms. Nothing is a difficult word to define. Let's say nothing is "something" with no properties. But if nothing has no properties, it has the property of having no properties. Nothing cannot be described, but even then nothing gains the property of being unable to be described. Nothing is nothing, no, not even, for that gains the property of being nothing. The most extreme definition of nothing is not a definition, and it isn't not a definition. Nothing is nothing, and nothing isn't nothing. It is also not anything in between. 

Nothing does not occupy space, does not occupy time, does not occupy definition.

## Hard Determinism

Let's say I knew every conceivable thing about your life. Every decision you have ever made in the past, every experience you've ever had. No, in fact, let's go a little farther. Let's say I not only knew all of those things, but I knew the [position, velocity, acceleration, jerk, snap, crackle, pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth,_fifth,_and_sixth_derivatives_of_position) of every single [subatomic particle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subatomic_particle) within every single molecule within every single cell in your body. 

Will I be able to, given these parameters, predict the position, velocity, acceleration, jerk, snap, crackle, pop of every single subatomic particle in your body in exactly the next [planck time](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_time)?
